                                    Description
Title                          :        WHO AM I?
Team members      :        2 members per team    
Rules and regulations:
Strictly 2 members per team allowed.
Usage of mobile phones, smartwatches, or internet is strictly prohibited.
Any form of cheating, external help, or audience hinting will lead to immediate disqualification.
The decision of the coordinators/judges will be final and binding.
Number of rounds:  3 Rounds
Detailed Description:
Round 1 : LOGO HUNT
Rules :
Time Limit: 30 seconds per question.
No discussion between participants allowed.
Task:
Identify the correct original logo out of 4–6 options displayed on screen.
Winner Calculation :
Points for each correct question is calculated (10 points per question).
No negative marking.
Teams selected in round 1 will be shortlisted for round 2.
Round 2: GUESS  BY EMOJI
Rules :
Time Limit: 45 seconds per question.
Must press the buzzer/bell first to get the chance to answer.
Task:
Connect the set of emojis on screen and guess the correct persona/character.
Winner Calculation :
+10 points for correct answer.
-5 penalty points for wrong answer or false buzzer.
Round 3: 3 HINTS-1 PERSONA
Rules :
Teams can press the buzzer at any hint stage.
Time Limit: 45 seconds per question.
Maximum 1 guess per team per persona.
Task:
Guess the hidden persona using up to 3 progressive clues (Hard to Easy).
Winner Calculation :
Guess at Hint 1: +30 Points
Guess at Hint 2: +20 Points
Guess at Hint 3: +10 Points
Penalty: -10 points for a wrong guess.
The team with the highest total score wins the event!

Problem Statement
The problem statement will be announced at the start of the event and is the same for all participants/teams.
The problem statement will be shared only at the start of the event — no prior access or hints will be given
Participants must carefully read and analyze the problem statement before starting the design process
The design must directly address the given problem — solutions unrelated to the problem statement will not be considered for judging
Participants may make reasonable assumptions where the problem statement is open-ended, but assumptions must be clearly mentioned during the presentation
Doubts or clarifications regarding the problem statement will be addressed only during the announced clarification window at the start of the event
No changes to the problem statement will be entertained once the event has started
Interpretation of the problem statement is at the sole discretion of the organizers/judges
AI Usage
AI tools are allowed only for the following purposes:
Research, inspiration, and brainstorming
Generating content/copy (placeholder text, labels)
Explaining concepts or getting design suggestions
AI tools must NOT be used to:
Auto-generate full screens, layouts, or complete UI designs
Copy AI-generated design templates directly into the submission
Other Guidelines
1. Team Size — Individual participation or teams of up to 2 members are allowed.
2. Tools — Only Figma is allowed for this event.
3. Submission — Final design/prototype must be submitted before the deadline.
4. Judging — Based on UX, UI, creativity, innovation, user flow, and presentation.
5. Decision — Judges' decision is final and binding.
By registering for this event, all participants agree to abide by the above rules and regulations.

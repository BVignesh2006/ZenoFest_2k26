Title: Rapid Fire
Team Size: 2-3 participants
Rules and Regulations:
The event can be conducted in teams.
Questions will be based on Technology, Apps, Social Media, Entertainment and General Knowledge.
No mobile phones, internet, smartwatches, or external help are allowed.
Once an answer is given, it cannot be changed.
Participants can say “Pass” if they don’t know an answer.
In case of a tie, a Tie-Breaker Round will be conducted.
The decision of the judges will be final.
Number of Rounds: 3 Rounds
Detailed Description:
Round 1 – Fun Rapid Fire
Rules:
Each team will be given 30 seconds.
Fun and interesting questions will be asked.
Task:
Answer one  question within 30 seconds.
Winner Calculation:
Each correct answer = 1 point
Total points will be added to the overall score.
Top 4 teams will be shortlisted for Round 2.
Round 2 – Think Fast
Rules:
  Each team will be given 45 seconds. 
  Questions will be slightly challenging.
Task:
   Answer the questions correctly as quickly as possible.
Winner Calculation:
 Each correct answer = 2 Points 
 Points will be added to the Round 1 score.
 Based on the Round 2 performance, Top 2 teams will be selected        for Round 3.
Round 3 – Final Challenge
Rules:
  Each team will be given 60 seconds. 
  Challenging rapid-fire questions will be asked.
Task:
Answer maximum questions correctly within 60 seconds.
Winner Calculation:
 Each correct answer = 3 Points. 
 The 2 finalist teams will compete in the Final Challenge. 
 The team with the highest score/performance in Round 3 will be  declared the Winner.
